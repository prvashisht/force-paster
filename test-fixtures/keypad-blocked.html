On-screen keypad test page

Modelled on a bank login (AU Bank's netbanking). The field blocks paste, the page disables right-click, and while the on-screen keypad is open every key press is cancelled, so Ctrl+V never even becomes a paste. With Force Paster OFF, nothing pastes. With it ON, Ctrl/Cmd+V, Shift+Insert and right-click → Paste should all work, keypad open or not.
